Emission Factor Data Ingestion

Architected a robust data ingestion pipeline that strictly separates the Control Plane (subscription-based access) from the Data Plane (customer-hosted databases) to securely distribute climate and sustainability emission factors.

Overview

In enterprise carbon accounting, accessing accurate and up-to-date emission factors is critical. However, emission factor datasets are highly sensitive, often licensed, and access must be strictly controlled based on what specific climate and sustainability packages a client is subscribed to.

I designed and implemented a specialized ingestion pipeline to handle this. By utilizing a Control Plane / Data Plane architecture, I ensured that ingestion logic and subscription validation remain centralized, while the actual data resides securely within the customer's isolated environment.

Architecture Highlights

  • Control Plane: Manages the ingestion of raw emission factors. It evaluates client subscription levels (opt-ins for specific climate databases) and orchestrates which datasets are pushed to which tenants.
  • Data Plane: The customer-hosted environment. The Control Plane securely pushes validated emission factors into a customer-specific PostgreSQL database.
  • Service Integration: Various microservices (like the Carbon Engine and Reporting tools) operate entirely within the Data Plane, accessing the local PostgreSQL instance for low-latency carbon accounting calculations.
  • Data Security: Ensures total data isolation. Customer databases only contain the emission factors they are entitled to, completely preventing cross-tenant data leaks.
